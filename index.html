LESSON 01 • GRAMMAR

“To be” fe’li: am / is / are

“To be” kimdir yoki nimadir kimligini, qandayligini aytishda ishlatiladi.

I am a student. — Men o‘quvchiman.

She is ready. — U tayyor.

They are students. — Ular o‘quvchilar.

O‘zgaruvchi nima?
